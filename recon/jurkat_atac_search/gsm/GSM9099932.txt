!Sample_treatment_protocol_ch1 = For CRISPRa, gRNAs were clone into lentiSAMv216 (containing blasticidin selection marker, Addgene #75112)(Joung et al. 2017), which contains dCas9-VP64 and MS2 binding loop in the sgRNA backbone to recruit activation domains p65 and HSF1 (encoded on the Lenti-MPHv2 vector). This synergistic activation mediator (SAM) complex increases dCas9-VP64 mediated gRNA-specific activation. For CRISPRi, gRNAs were cloned into pCRISPRia-v2 (containing puromycin selection marker, Addgene #84832)(Horlbeck et al. 2016). Two days after transduction, cells underwent antibiotic selection for at least 1 week with 100 μg/mL hygromycin and 8 μg/mL blasticidin was used for CRISPRa and gRNA-transduced Jurkat T cell clones and 8 μg/mL blasticidin and 1.5 μg/mL puromycin for CRISPRi and gRNA transduced Jurkat T cell clones.
!Sample_growth_protocol_ch1 = Cell lines were cultured in RPMI 1640 (Thermo Fisher Scientific 11875093) supplemented with 10% heat-inactivated fetal bovine serum (Thermo Fisher Scientific 10438026). Two antibiotics were added to select for each of gRNA and dCas9 fusion protein transduction.
!Sample_molecule_ch1 = genomic DNA
!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = Approximately 200,000 cells from each Jurkat T cell clone underwent Ficoll density  gradient centrifugation for dead cell removal (Ficoll-Paque PLUS Medium, VWR #95038-168). Approximately 50,000 cells per aliquot (3 replicates per Jurkat T cell clone) were used for ATAC-seq and 50,000 cells per aliquot for RNA-seq. For ATAC-seq, aliquots 50,000 cells were resuspended in 50 µL ATAC-lysis buffer (10 mM Tris-HCl at pH 7.4, 10 mM NaCl, 3 mM MgCl2, 0.1% Tween-20, 0.1% NP-40, 0.01% digitonin) on ice for 3 min. We then diluted the mixture with 1mL ATAC-wash (10 mM Tris-HCl at pH 7.4, 10 mM NaCl, 3 mM MgCl2, 0.1% Tween-20). Cells were washed and pelleted then transposed for 30 min at 37˚C (1× TD buffer with 33% PBS, 0.1% Tween-20, 0.01% digitonin, 2.5 µL TDE1). After transposition, DNA was isolated using Qiagen MinElute PCR cleanup kit, #28004.
!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = Library prep was performed using NEBNext Ultra II Q5 master mix (NEB #M0544X) and Nextera indexed according to NEBNext Ultra II (NEB #E7645) instructions. Following baseline amplification, typically 8 cycles, SYBR qPCR was used to determine total cycle numbers needed. Typically, a total of 12 additional PCR cycles of library amplification were added, depending on qPCR results. After validating library size on an Agilent TapeStation, 150-bp paired-end sequencing was performed by NovaSeq 6000 at Yale Center for Genome Sequencing.

!Sample_data_processing = We called ATAC peaks with Genrich (https://github.com/jsh58/Genrich) arguments “-j -y -r -e chrM -v” as in (Collora and Ho 2023) after Samtools (Li et al. 2009)(v1.17) was used to sort and index the mapped reads. !Sample_data_processing = Duplicate reads were identified with Piccard’s (v3.0.0; https://broadinstitute.github.io/picard/; Broad Institute) markduplicates function.
!Sample_data_processing = For each set of samples to be directly compared (that is, the gene-activating or gene-inhibiting samples for a given integration site), Samtools was used to down-sample reads without replacement to the lowest molecular complexity among the compared samples. The Piccard-identified duplicate reads were then removed.
!Sample_data_processing = We quantified reads in peaks with Bedtools multicov and generated coverage data in a Bigwig file at 10 nt resolution with the Deeptools (v3.5) bamCoverage function (Ramirez et al. 2014), normalizing to CPM (generating processed data .bw files). Tracks for plotting were also obtained with the ATACorrect function from TOBIAS. "Peaks" were added corresponding to 5kb on each side of the HIV integration sites to quantify HIV impact on accessibility.
!Sample_data_processing = Normalization of peak counts was performed by NormOffsets function from csaw, specifically between samples being compared at the time of differential expression analysis, so we provide the original counts of reads mapping to peaks that would be used in each comparison (followed by edgeR for differential accessibility analysis).
